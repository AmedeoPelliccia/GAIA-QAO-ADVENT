# IDEALE E₂ Economy — Governed Industrial Digital Economy

> *"Without E₂, evidence is produced but never valued. With E₂, uncertainty
> reduction has a price, circularity has a market, and contribution has a wallet.
> The sixth pillar makes the other five self-sustaining."*

## Overview

This directory contains the specifications for **IDEALE Pillar E₂ — Economy (Digital)**,
the sixth and final pillar of the IDEALE framework. E₂ provides the circulation system
that makes the entire IDEALE organism self-sustaining through governed marketplaces,
ethical market rules, and blockchain-anchored settlement.
